Get Jitter

Jitter runs as an app on your Home Screen. Add it once, then open it from there.

  1. 1Tap Share in Safari's toolbar
  2. 2Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  3. 3Tap Add, then open Jitter from your Home Screen

Using Chrome or another browser? Open this page in Safari first.
